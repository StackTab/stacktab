Global Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 10, 2026

1. Our Privacy Philosophy

At StackTab, we build privacy-first developer utilities and productivity extensions. We believe that tools should run on your hardware without monitoring your behavior or harvesting your private browsing data.

Nothing. None of our extensions (including TabIntel and Path Jump) collect, transmit, monetize, or log your personal data, web activity, or cookies. All logic runs 100% locally on your machine.
