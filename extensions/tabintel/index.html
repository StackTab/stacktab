FAQ

Common questions

How does TabIntel detect duplicate tabs?

TabIntel compares open tab URLs. In "Exact URL" mode, it matches the full URL. In "Domain" mode, it compares only the hostname, so github.com/one and github.com/two would be flagged as duplicates.

Which tab is kept when duplicates are removed?

You can choose to keep either the newest or oldest tab. The rest of the copies are closed.

Can I group duplicates instead of closing them?

Yes. If you don't want to close duplicates immediately, you can choose to group them together into a Chrome Tab Group.

How does tab search work?

When you open TabIntel, you can instantly start typing to search the visible text (titles and URLs) across all your open tabs, and quickly jump to the one you need.

Does TabIntel send any data anywhere?

No. Tab information is stored and processed locally on your device. There are no ads, no accounts required, and no browsing data transmitted to external servers.

Can I change the keyboard shortcut?

Yes. Go to chrome://extensions/shortcuts (or edge://extensions/shortcuts on Edge) and reassign the shortcut to any key combination you prefer.
