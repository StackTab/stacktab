Privacy Policy for TabIntel

Last Updated: October 10, 2026

1. Overview & Single Purpose

TabIntel (offered by StackTab) is designed with a single, clear purpose: to help users find, search, and clean duplicate browser tabs locally on their device.

2. Zero Data Collection & Local-First Architecture

We strongly believe your browsing activity is private to you. TabIntel operates on a 100% local-first architecture:

  • No Telemetry: We do not track what websites you visit, how many tabs you open, or what terms you search for.
  • No Remote Servers: TabIntel does not communicate with any external backend servers, third-party analytics (e.g., Google Analytics, Mixpanel), or ad networks.
  • No Personal Information: We never collect, store, transmit, or sell personal data of any kind.

3. Purpose of Requested Permissions

To provide its core functionality, TabIntel requests the following browser permissions in accordance with browser extension store policies:

Permission How It Is Used
tabs Used exclusively to read open tab URLs, titles, and IDs on your device so the extension can detect duplicate tabs and allow you to switch to or close them. This data remains solely in volatile memory during the active session.
storage Used solely to store your chosen cleanup preferences locally in Chrome sync storage.

4. Third-Party Disclosures

TabIntel does not sell, trade, rent, or transfer your data to outside parties. Because no data is collected, there is no data to share.

5. Compliance with Store Policies

TabIntel complies with the Google Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies (including the User Data FAQ and Limited Use requirements) and Microsoft Edge Add-ons policies.
